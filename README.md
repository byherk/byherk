# Hi, I'm HerK

I'm an independent researcher exploring how AI, human expertise, and real-world evidence could support learning and career discovery.

My broader interests include operations, process improvement, AI-enabled workflows, human-in-the-loop systems, and evidence-informed decision making.

## Current project

### [Career Evidence Loop](https://github.com/byherk/career-evidence-loop)

> What if career fit were treated as a hypothesis to test, rather than a verdict to accept?

Career Evidence Loop is an open research project examining how learners, educators, career counselors, domain experts, and AI systems might gather and update evidence about capabilities and possible career directions over time.

The project is built around a simple principle:

> **Don't tell learners who they are. Help them gather enough evidence to discover what they could become.**

Current work focuses on research mapping, evidence and career-hypothesis models, human-in-the-loop evaluation, and questions around uncertainty, bias, ethics, and learner agency.

The project is still at an early stage. I published it before the ideas were fully settled because I would rather have weak assumptions challenged early than make them look more certain than they are.

Research, critiques, counterexamples, and perspectives from different professions are welcome.

> **Strong counterarguments are contributions too.**
