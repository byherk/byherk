## Current project

### [Career Evidence Loop](https://github.com/byherk/career-evidence-loop)

> How can we test career possibilities without turning incomplete evidence into labels about who someone is?

Career Evidence Loop is an independent open research project exploring how learners, educators, career counselors, domain experts, and AI systems might gather and revise evidence about learning and possible career directions over time.

The central idea is:

> **Career fit may be more useful as a hypothesis to test than a verdict to accept.**

Current work focuses on problem framing, research mapping, evidence and career-hypothesis models, human-in-the-loop evaluation, and questions around uncertainty, bias, ethics, and learner agency.

The project is still at an early research stage. I’m learning from established research, making assumptions and limitations visible, and inviting critique from people with relevant expertise.

Research references, critiques, counterexamples, practitioner perspectives, and other contributions are welcome.

> **Don't tell learners who they are. Help them gather enough evidence to discover what they could become.**
